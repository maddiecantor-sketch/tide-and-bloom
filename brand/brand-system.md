# Tide & Bloom Brand System

## Positioning

Tide & Bloom is a modern luxury coastal lifestyle brand built around coordinated resortwear, swimwear, family matching sets, apparel, accessories, and home goods.

## Brand Mood

- Feminine
- Editorial
- Coastal
- Resort-inspired
- Bohemian
- Modern
- Sun-washed
- Premium but approachable

## Core Palette

- Cream — `#FBF8F3`
- Sand — `#E8DDCE`
- Blush Pink — `#F4C2C2`
- Coral — `#D98868`
- Butter Yellow — `#F0D98A`
- Seafoam — `#BFD8D2`
- Sky Blue — `#C9DCE8`
- Ocean Navy — `#1D3557`
- Warm Ink — `#2B2620`

## Typography Direction

### Display Serif
Use an elegant, high-contrast serif for hero headlines, campaign titles, collection names, and editorial statements.

Suggested direction: Cormorant Garamond, DM Serif Display, or a licensed premium editorial serif.

### Sans Serif
Use a clean geometric or humanist sans serif for navigation, product information, buttons, filters, and body copy.

Suggested direction: Jost, Manrope, or Inter.

## Photography Direction

- Natural sun and soft shadow
- Warm coastal environments
- Boutique hotel, pool, beach, boat, boardwalk, and airy home settings
- Editorial crops with movement and imperfect styling
- Avoid generic stock-photo energy
- Keep product colors accurate and prints recognizable

## Illustration Direction

- Soft watercolor fills
- Fine hand-drawn outlines
- Consistent line weight
- Sun-faded pastel color
- Slight texture and paper grain
- Motifs should feel collected from travel, not clip-art based

## UI Direction

- Spacious editorial layouts
- Rounded corners used selectively
- Soft shadows
- Linen and watercolor texture accents
- Large immersive imagery
- Elegant product cards
- Mobile-first interactions
- Subtle, slow animation instead of aggressive effects

## Voice

Tide & Bloom copy should feel warm, polished, playful, and vacation-minded.

### Use

- Sunny
- Effortless
- Collected
- Coastal
- Made to match
- Resort-ready
- Limited edition

### Avoid

- Overly corporate language
- Cheap or discount-heavy positioning
- Generic beach quotes
- Excessive slang in customer-facing copy

## Tagline

**Made for Sunny Days & Salty Waves**
