# Amalfi Citrus Club Launch Plan

## Launch Goal

Use Collection 01 as the proof-of-system launch for Tide & Bloom: original artwork, coordinated products, luxury mockups, storefront merchandising, and a repeatable content engine.

## Core Message

**Made for Sunny Days & Salty Waves**

Amalfi Citrus Club is a sun-washed Italian-coast collection built around lemons, painted tile, coral bows, postcards, and relaxed resort color.

## Launch Assets Required

- 1 hero campaign image
- 6 product mockups
- 3 family-matching group images
- 3 vertical Reels/TikTok videos
- 6 Pinterest pins
- 10 feed posts
- 15 story frames
- 3 launch emails
- 1 collection landing page
- 1 size/fit guide
- 1 shipping and returns FAQ

## Content Sequence

### Tease
- Color palette reveal
- Cropped motif previews
- "Postcard from somewhere sunny" story series
- Waitlist signup

### Reveal
- Full hero print
- Collection name and story
- Product-category preview
- Matching-family announcement

### Launch
- Hero campaign video
- Product carousel
- Founder/brand-story post
- Email announcement
- Limited launch incentive

### Sustain
- Styling ideas
- Product close-ups
- Customer photos and reviews
- Vacation packing bundles
- Best-seller restock content

## Short-Form Video Formula

1. Hook: visual surprise in first 2 seconds
2. Product story: what makes the collection different
3. Proof: close-up, fit, matching pieces, or mockup transformation
4. Call to action: shop, join waitlist, or save launch date

## Initial Offer Options

Choose one for launch testing:

- Free U.S. shipping over $100
- 10% off first order for email signup
- Matching-family bundle savings
- Free sticker pack with first 100 orders

## Measurement

Track:

- Email signup conversion
- Product-page conversion
- Add-to-cart rate
- Average order value
- Gross margin by product
- Top content by saves and clicks
- Best-selling size and category
