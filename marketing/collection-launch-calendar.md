## Standard Eight-Week Launch Cycle

- **Week 1:** collection story, motif approval, palette lock
- **Week 2:** hero print and collection logo approval
- **Week 3:** coordinate prints and placement graphics
- **Week 4:** supplier mapping and first mockups
- **Week 5:** physical sample orders and storefront copy
- **Week 6:** sample review, photography, pricing lock
- **Week 7:** teaser campaign, email waitlist, creator seeding
- **Week 8:** launch, paid-content tests, customer feedback

No collection advances to launch until its production QA checklist is complete.
